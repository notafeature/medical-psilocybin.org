Medical psilocybin in New Mexico

An independent community record of the state's medical psilocybin program: the rules as the Department of Health publishes them, what each requires of the people it reaches, the statute they implement, and the record of how each came to be. Built from public documents, cited to the page, free to read, and affiliated with no agency, company, or organization.

New Mexico enacted the Medical Psilocybin Act in 2025. The department adopted its first rule, for producers and testing laboratories, on June 23, 2026, and proposed its second, for patients, clinicians, practitioners, facilitators, healing centers, and educational programs, with a rule hearing fixed for October 2, 2026. The program must be implemented by December 31, 2027.
